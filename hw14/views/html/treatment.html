Our Magical Treatments

We care for your little ones' teeth with lots of love and zero pain.

Treatment What it consists of Post-Treatment Suggestions
Prophylaxis and Dental Cleaning Gentle cleaning to remove bacterial plaque and reinforce correct brushing techniques. Avoid highly sugary foods the same day and maintain brushing after every meal.
Fluoride Application (Varnish) Application of fluoride varnish on teeth to strengthen enamel and prevent cavities. Do not eat or drink for 30 minutes and avoid hard foods for the first few hours.
Pit and Fissure Sealants Placement of a protective layer in the grooves of molars to prevent food accumulation. Avoid chewing very sticky things the first day and attend the semiannual check-up.
Restorations (Fillings) Removal of cavities and reconstruction of the tooth with high-quality aesthetic materials. Avoid hard foods for 24 hours and report any intense sensitivity or prolonged pain.
Interceptive Orthodontics Early correction of alterations in maxillary growth or dental position to guide the bite. Comply with the use of the appliance as indicated, maintain rigorous hygiene and attend check-ups.
